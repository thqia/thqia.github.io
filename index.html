Why are butterflies so colorful?

Well, it's actually an adaptation for them. How is it an adaptation? The color of butterflies can indicate that they are poisonous or bitterly tasting to their predators. Their color can also help them camoflauge with their enviornment if predators are nearby.

Butterfly Metamorphosis

First, the adult butterfly will lay her eggs on a leaf. After a few days, the tiny eggs will hatch into butterfly larvae (caterpillars). These caterpillars will munch on leaves until about 5 weeks. Then they get huge and need to make their chrysalis. After a week or two, the chrysalis will become transparent and the grown butterfly will poke out of the chrysalis with their wings. The butterfly's wings will be small and wet wheras their skinny body will look huge. The butterfly will rest on the chrysalis until its wings are dry and big and their body is skinny. The butterfly will only live for another two to three weeks before reproduction and death.

What the butterfly eats

Butterflies eat the raw nectar from plants using their long tongues.

Types of butterflies

There are many different types of butterflies. One of the most known butterfly types is the Monarch butterfly (found in North and South America). There are many other butterflies, such as the Swallowtail butterfly (can be found anywhere except Antarctica). As a caterpillar, the Black Swallowtail has an horn-like orange osmeterium that is used for defense against predators. Another type of butterfly is the Postman. It is found in Central and Sputh America, mainly Costa Rica. It has red patterns on its wings that indicate that it is posionous.

Predators of butterflies

The most common predator of butterflies are birds. Birds mainly eat insects, and they like feasting on insects such as caterpillars and butterflies. Believe it or not, we are also predators of butterflies. We destroy their habitat by building things. Do you know of the Crossgates Mall? Actually, Karner blue butterflies used to live in that area before people decided to build a mall there. Thus, by constructing the mall, the Karner blue butterflies lost their habitat. These butterflies are endangered at the moment.
